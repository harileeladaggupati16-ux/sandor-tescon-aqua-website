Frequently Asked Questions

We have answered some common questions about our water and wastewater treatment solutions. If you have a specific requirement, our team can help you identify a suitable treatment approach.

A water treatment system is a combination of treatment processes used to improve water quality for a specific application. Depending on the water source and required quality, treatment may include filtration, RO, UV, ozone or other suitable processes.

An RO plant uses reverse osmosis technology to reduce dissolved salts and other unwanted substances from water. RO systems can be designed for suitable commercial, institutional and industrial applications.

STP stands for Sewage Treatment Plant. It is designed to treat sewage generated from applications such as residential communities, commercial buildings and institutions before suitable discharge or reuse.

ETP stands for Effluent Treatment Plant. It is used for treating wastewater generated from industrial or process operations. The treatment configuration depends on the type and characteristics of the effluent.

Solutions can include RO systems, filtration systems, STP, ETP, wastewater treatment, UV treatment and ozone treatment depending on the application and treatment requirement.

A filtration system removes suspended particles and other unwanted materials from water. Filtration can be used as a standalone treatment or as pre-treatment for other processes.

UV water treatment uses ultraviolet light as a disinfection process. It can be considered for suitable water treatment applications where UV disinfection is appropriate.

Ozone treatment uses ozone as an oxidation and treatment process. It can be incorporated into suitable water treatment systems depending on the required treatment objective.

The appropriate system depends on factors such as water source, water quality, required capacity, treatment objective and operating requirements. These factors should be reviewed before selecting the treatment technology.
