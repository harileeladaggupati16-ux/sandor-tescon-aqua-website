Why Sewage Treatment Is Important

Proper sewage treatment helps protect water resources, public health and the environment.

Water Reuse

Properly treated wastewater can be reused for suitable non-potable applications such as gardening, landscaping, and toilet flushing.

Environmental Protection

STP systems help reduce the release of untreated sewage into the environment and water bodies.

Resource Recovery

Treated wastewater can help reduce freshwater consumption when reused for appropriate applications.

Sewage Treatment Process

A typical sewage treatment system may include multiple treatment stages.

1. Preliminary Treatment

Screening and removal of large solids and unwanted materials.

2. Biological Treatment

Biological processes help reduce biodegradable organic matter.

3. Clarification

Separation of suspended solids from the treated wastewater.

4. Final Treatment

Additional treatment can be provided depending on the required water quality.
