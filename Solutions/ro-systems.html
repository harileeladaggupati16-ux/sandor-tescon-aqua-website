RO PROCESS

How Reverse OsmosisWorks

01
Pre-Treatment

Feed water passes through suitable pre-treatment stages to reduce suspended solids and protect downstream equipment.

02
High-Pressure Pump

A high-pressure pump provides the pressure required to push water through the RO membrane.

03
RO Membrane

The RO membrane separates dissolved salts and other contaminants from the feed water.

04
Treated Water

Treated permeate is collected for the intended application according to the required water-quality specification.
