TREATMENT PROCESS

How Wastewater Treatment Works

A wastewater treatment plant may use multiple treatment stages depending on the wastewater composition and required treated-water quality.

01
Preliminary Treatment

Screening and preliminary separation help remove larger solids and unwanted materials from incoming wastewater.

02
Primary Treatment

Physical treatment processes such as settling and separation help remove settleable and floating materials.

03
Biological Treatment

Biological processes use microorganisms to reduce biodegradable organic matter in wastewater.

04
Tertiary Treatment

Additional filtration and disinfection stages may be provided to achieve the required treated-water quality.
