FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about water treatment systems, equipment, installation, and maintenance.

The right treatment system depends on factors such as the source of water, water quality, flow rate, required treatment level, and intended application. A proper water analysis helps determine the suitable treatment approach.

RO systems are commonly used for removing dissolved salts and other impurities from water. STPs are designed to treat sewage wastewater, while ETPs are designed to treat industrial effluent. The appropriate system depends on the characteristics of the water or wastewater being treated.

System design generally considers water quality, required capacity, treatment objectives, operating conditions, available space, and applicable requirements. Water analysis and application requirements are important inputs for selecting suitable treatment technologies.

Installation and commissioning can be included depending on the project scope and the services offered for the selected system. Project-specific requirements can be discussed during the quotation and planning stage.

Maintenance frequency depends on the type of system, operating hours, water quality, equipment condition, and usage. Regular inspection and maintenance of filters, membranes, pumps, instruments, and other components can help maintain system performance.

Technical support can be provided according to the project scope and service requirements. Contact the Sandor Tescon Aqua team to discuss your application, equipment requirements, and service needs.
