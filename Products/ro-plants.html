PROCESS

How an RO PlantWorks

01
Pre-Treatment

Incoming water can pass through appropriate pre-treatment stages to reduce suspended solids and protect downstream equipment.

02
High-Pressure Pump

A high-pressure pump supplies the pressure required to drive water through the RO membrane.

03
RO Membrane

The membrane separates a portion of dissolved salts and other contaminants from the feed water.

04
Treated Water

The treated permeate can then be used according to the required application and water-quality specification.
