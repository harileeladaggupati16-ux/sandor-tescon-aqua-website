OZONE TREATMENT PROCESS

How an Ozone SystemWorks

An ozone treatment system generates ozone and introduces it into the required treatment stream through a suitable injection and contact arrangement.

01
Ozone Generation

Ozone is generated on-site using an ozone generator with a suitable feed gas and operating arrangement.

02
Ozone Injection

Generated ozone is introduced into the water stream using a suitable injector or gas transfer system.

03
Ozone Contact

Ozone comes into contact with water in a suitable contact arrangement to support the required treatment process.

04
Treated Water Outlet

Treated water proceeds to the next treatment stage according to the overall plant configuration.
