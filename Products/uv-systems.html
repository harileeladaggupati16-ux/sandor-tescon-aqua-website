DISINFECTION PROCESS

How a UV SystemWorks

A UV water disinfection system exposes flowing water to ultraviolet light inside a treatment chamber.

01
Water Inlet

Water enters the UV treatment unit after the required upstream filtration or pre-treatment.

02
UV Exposure

Water flows through the treatment chamber and is exposed to ultraviolet light from the UV lamp.

03
Microbial Inactivation

UV light damages the genetic material of susceptible microorganisms, preventing their replication.

04
Treated Water Outlet

Treated water leaves the UV chamber for the next stage or intended application.
