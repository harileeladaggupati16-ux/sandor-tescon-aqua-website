WATER SOFTENING SYSTEMS

Effective Treatment for Hard Water

Water softeners are used to reduce hardness caused primarily by dissolved calcium and magnesium ions in water.

Sandor Tescon Aqua provides water softening solutions that can be configured for commercial and industrial applications according to feed water quality, flow rate, hardness level and treatment requirements.

Depending on the application, water softeners can be integrated with filtration, pre-treatment, storage tanks, pumps and other water treatment equipment.

Hardness Reduction

Water softening systems can help reduce calcium and magnesium hardness, supporting equipment protection and improving the suitability of water for various commercial and industrial applications.

SYSTEM COMPONENTS

Key Components of a Water Softener

A water softening system can include resin, control equipment, brine regeneration and supporting filtration according to the application requirements.

Softener Vessel

The softener vessel contains ion-exchange resin through which hard water passes for hardness reduction.

Ion Exchange Resin

Ion-exchange resin is used to exchange hardness-forming calcium and magnesium ions with sodium ions during the softening process.

Automatic Control Valve

Automatic control valves can manage service, regeneration, backwash and rinse cycles according to the selected system configuration.

Brine Tank

The brine tank provides the salt solution required for regeneration of the ion-exchange resin.

Pre-Filtration

Suitable pre-filtration can be incorporated to reduce suspended matter and protect downstream water softening equipment.

Monitoring & Control

Flow, pressure and operating parameters can be monitored to support convenient system operation and maintenance.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Designed Around Your Water Quality Requirements

The selection of a water softener depends on water hardness, flow requirement, regeneration frequency and the intended application.

  • Raw water source and water quality
  • Total hardness level
  • Required treated water flow rate
  • Daily water consumption
  • Required regeneration frequency
  • Available installation space

Water Hardness

Softener sizing can be considered according to the hardness level and characteristics of the source water.

Flow Requirement

The system can be selected according to the required service flow and water demand.

Regeneration

Regeneration settings can be planned according to resin capacity and operating conditions.

Automatic Operation

Automatic control systems can support scheduled regeneration and convenient system operation.
