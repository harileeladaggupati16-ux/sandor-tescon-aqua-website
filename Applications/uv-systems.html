UV WATER TREATMENT

Advanced Ultraviolet Disinfection Technology

Ultraviolet (UV) water treatment is a disinfection process that uses UV-C light to inactivate microorganisms in water. It provides a physical disinfection method without requiring the addition of chemical disinfectants.

Sandor Tescon Aqua provides UV water treatment solutions for drinking water systems, commercial facilities, industrial processes and suitable wastewater treatment applications.

UV systems can be selected according to water flow rate, UV transmittance, water quality, required UV dose and operating conditions.

UV Disinfection Technology

UV-C radiation is used to inactivate microorganisms as water passes through a specially designed UV chamber. Proper system sizing and operating conditions are essential for effective disinfection.

UV SYSTEM COMPONENTS

Key Components of UV Systems

A UV disinfection system incorporates specialized components to provide controlled ultraviolet treatment and reliable operation.

UV Reactor Chamber

The UV chamber provides a controlled flow passage where water is exposed to ultraviolet radiation for disinfection.

UV-C Lamps

UV-C lamps generate ultraviolet radiation used for microorganism inactivation during the treatment process.

Quartz Sleeves

Quartz sleeves protect UV lamps from direct water contact while allowing ultraviolet radiation to pass into the treatment chamber.

Control Panel

Electrical control panels support UV system operation, lamp control and monitoring functions.

Electronic Ballast

Electronic ballast units provide the required electrical power for UV lamp operation.

Monitoring System

UV intensity sensors and monitoring equipment can be incorporated according to the required system configuration.

UV TREATMENT PROCESS

How UV Water Disinfection Works

UV water treatment uses ultraviolet radiation to inactivate microorganisms as water flows through a specially designed disinfection chamber.

01
Water Pre-Treatment

Water is suitably filtered to reduce suspended particles and improve UV transmission.

02
UV Exposure

Water passes through the UV reactor chamber and is exposed to UV-C radiation.

03
Microorganism Inactivation

UV-C radiation damages the genetic material of susceptible microorganisms, preventing their replication.

04
Treated Water

Disinfected water moves to the next treatment stage or point of use.

SYSTEM SELECTION

Selecting the Right UV Disinfection System

Proper UV system selection is important for achieving the required disinfection performance. The system should be designed based on the actual water characteristics and operating requirements.

  • Required water flow rate
  • Water quality and UV transmittance
  • Required UV disinfection dose
  • Operating pressure and temperature
  • Installation and piping requirements
  • UV lamp and quartz sleeve maintenance
  • Monitoring and control requirements
