DIALYSIS RO PLANT

Water Treatment for Dialysis Applications

Dialysis treatment requires appropriately treated water as part of the overall dialysis water system. Water treatment configurations are selected according to source water characteristics, required capacity and applicable treatment requirements.

Sandor Tescon Aqua provides water treatment solutions incorporating pre-treatment, filtration, reverse osmosis and suitable disinfection processes for dialysis applications.

The treatment system can be configured according to the water quality, dialysis unit requirements, operating conditions and site-specific requirements.

Dialysis Water Treatment

Integrated treatment systems can combine filtration, reverse osmosis, disinfection and treated water storage to support suitable dialysis water treatment applications.

SYSTEM COMPONENTS

Key Dialysis RO Plant Systems

A dialysis water treatment system can incorporate multiple treatment stages selected according to source water quality, required capacity and treatment objectives.

Pre-Treatment

Pre-treatment systems can reduce suspended particles and other contaminants before water enters downstream purification stages.

Water Filtration

Suitable filtration stages can be incorporated to improve feed water quality and protect downstream treatment equipment.

Reverse Osmosis

Reverse osmosis provides a key purification stage for reducing dissolved substances and producing treated water.

UV Disinfection

UV treatment can be integrated into suitable water treatment configurations where additional microbial control is required.

Treated Water Storage

Treated water storage systems can support continuous operation and supply treated water to downstream dialysis processes.

Monitoring & Control

Monitoring and control equipment can be incorporated to support reliable operation and observation of important treatment parameters.

  • Disinfection and microbial control requirements
  • Treated water storage and distribution
  • Site conditions and available space
