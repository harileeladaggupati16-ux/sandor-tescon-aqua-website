INDUSTRIAL RO PLANTS

Industrial Reverse Osmosis for Water Treatment

Industrial RO plants are used for water purification applications where reduction of dissolved solids and production of treated water are required.

Sandor Tescon Aqua provides industrial reverse osmosis solutions that can be configured according to raw water characteristics, required treatment capacity and the intended industrial application.

Depending on the project, an industrial RO system can be integrated with suitable pre-treatment, filtration, dosing, membrane systems, post-treatment and treated water storage.

Efficient Water Purification

Industrial RO systems can be designed to provide treated water for process, manufacturing and other industrial applications based on specific water quality and capacity requirements.

PLANT COMPONENTS

Key Components of an Industrial RO Plant

Industrial RO plants generally combine pre-treatment, membrane separation and supporting process equipment according to the source water and treatment objectives.

Pre-Treatment

Pre-treatment systems can help reduce suspended particles, turbidity and other contaminants before water enters the RO membrane system.

Multi-Media Filtration

Media filtration can be incorporated to support removal of suspended matter and improve the quality of feed water supplied to downstream treatment.

Activated Carbon Filtration

Activated carbon filtration can be used in suitable applications to help reduce chlorine, organic compounds and other substances that may affect downstream treatment.

RO Membrane System

Reverse osmosis membranes provide the primary separation stage for reducing dissolved salts and producing purified water.

High-Pressure Pumping

High-pressure pumping equipment supplies the required pressure to the RO membrane system for effective membrane separation.

Control & Monitoring

Control panels and monitoring instruments can be integrated to support plant operation, monitoring and process control.

PLANT REQUIREMENTS

Designed Around Your Industrial Water Requirements

The configuration of an industrial RO plant depends on the raw water quality, required production capacity, desired treated water quality and overall application.

  • Raw water source and water quality
  • Required RO plant capacity
  • Required treated water quality
  • Feed water TDS and dissolved solids
  • Required pre-treatment stages
  • Available space and site conditions

Water Quality

Treatment design can be considered according to source water characteristics and quality requirements.

Pre-Treatment

Process Control
