JAR FILLING TECHNOLOGY

What is a 5 Liters Jar Filling Machine?

A 5 Liters Jar Filling Machine is designed to fill drinking water into 5 litre containers with a controlled and hygienic filling process. It is suitable for packaged drinking water production facilities, commercial water bottling units and other water packaging applications.

The filling system can be configured with suitable container handling, water filling and optional capping arrangements according to production requirements.

Sandor Tescon Aqua provides water bottling and filling equipment solutions based on plant capacity, container design and customer requirements.

  • Designed for 5 litre water containers
  • Suitable for packaged drinking water plants
  • Controlled and hygienic filling arrangement
  • Optional integration with container capping systems
MACHINE COMPONENTS

Key Components of Jar Filling Machine

The machine can incorporate different equipment and components to support container handling, water filling and packaging operations.

Water Filling Nozzles

Designed to deliver treated water into containers through a suitable filling arrangement.

Container Holding System

Supports proper positioning of 5 litre containers during the filling operation.

Conveyor Arrangement

Helps move containers through different stages of the filling and packaging process.

Filling Mechanism

Provides controlled water dispensing according to the selected filling system.

Control Panel

Allows operators to control and coordinate machine functions and operating settings.

Capping Arrangement

An optional capping unit can be integrated for closing filled containers.

WORKING PROCESS

How Does the Jar Filling Machine Work?

The filling process follows a sequence of container positioning, water filling and packaging operations.

01
Container Placement

Empty 5 litre containers are positioned in the designated filling area.

02
Water Filling

Treated drinking water is dispensed into containers through the filling nozzles.

03
Container Capping

Filled containers can be transferred for closure using a suitable capping arrangement.

04
Final Packaging

Filled and closed containers undergo inspection and are prepared for distribution.
