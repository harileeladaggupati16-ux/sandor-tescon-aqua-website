WATER JAR FILLING TECHNOLOGY

20 Litre Jar Washing, Filling and Capping Machine

A 20 litre jar filling machine is used in packaged drinking water plants to handle reusable water jars through washing, sanitizing, filling and capping operations.

Depending on the selected machine configuration, these operations can be arranged as individual or integrated stages to support an organized jar handling process.

Sandor Tescon Aqua offers water treatment and packaged drinking water plant solutions. Jar filling equipment can be selected according to production capacity, available space and operational requirements.

MACHINE FEATURES

Key Features of 20 Litre Jar Filling Machine

Equipment features depend on the selected machine model and automation level. Typical features may include:

Jar Washing System

Washing arrangements designed to clean reusable 20 litre jars before the filling operation.

Controlled Water Filling

Filling arrangements designed to deliver treated drinking water into the jars according to the selected machine configuration.

Hygienic Operation

Equipment layout designed to support hygienic handling of jars during washing and filling operations.

Automation Options

Manual, semi-automatic or automatic arrangements can be considered depending on production requirements.

Jar Handling

Jar loading, positioning and movement arrangements can be selected to suit the production line layout.

Capping Arrangement

Capping arrangements help close filled jars after the filling stage, according to the selected model.

MACHINE COMPONENTS

Main Parts of a Jar Filling Machine

A jar filling line can include different equipment sections to carry out the complete washing, filling and closing sequence.

Jar Loading Section

Provides the arrangement for loading and positioning empty jars before they enter the washing stage.

Jar Washing Section

Supports internal and external jar cleaning using the washing stages provided in the selected machine.

Filling Section

Transfers treated drinking water into cleaned jars through the configured filling arrangement.

Control Panel

Provides machine operation controls and supports coordination of the available machine functions.

Conveyor Arrangement

Supports movement of jars between machine sections where a conveyor system is included.

Capping Section

Provides the arrangement for placing and securing caps on filled jars.

WORKING PROCESS

How Does a 20 Litre Jar Filling Machine Work?

The operating sequence depends on the machine design and the selected level of automation.

01
Jar Loading

Empty reusable jars are loaded and positioned for the cleaning operation.

02
Jar Washing

Jars pass through the configured washing and rinsing stages.

03
Water Filling

Clean jars are filled with treated drinking water.

04
Capping & Collection

Filled jars are capped and moved to the collection area for further handling.
