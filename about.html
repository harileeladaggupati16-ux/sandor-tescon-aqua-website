Who We Are

Water Treatment & Engineering Solutions

Sandor Tescon Aqua focuses on water treatment and wastewater treatment solutions for different applications and operating requirements.

Our approach is centered on understanding water quality, process requirements and treatment objectives before selecting the appropriate treatment technologies.

Depending on the application, treatment solutions may include reverse osmosis, filtration, UV disinfection, ozone treatment, sewage treatment and effluent treatment.

Each project can be developed around the specific requirements of the application, available space, water quality and desired treated-water characteristics.

Application-Based Design

Treatment solutions can be selected according to project requirements.

Integrated Treatment

Multiple treatment technologies can be combined when required.

Technical Support

Project support can be structured according to the agreed scope.

Our Approach

From Water Analysis to Treatment

A systematic approach helps identify the right treatment process for each application.

01 / ANALYZE

02 / SELECT
